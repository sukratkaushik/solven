03 Expanded Product Suite for Buyers

1. Traffic-Light Risk Indicator ("ScoreCard")

An algorithmic risk score (0–100) combining three verifiable registry signals:

  • Governance Volatility: Frequent changes in managing directors within a 12-month window.
  • Transparency Lag: Has the company delayed publishing its statutory annual report by >18 months?
  • Capital Integrity: Any registered reductions in share capital or registered pledge agreements.

2. One-Click Due Diligence Dossier (PDF Export)

A boardroom-ready 3-page executive audit report downloadable in one click:

  • Full verified corporate structure & UBO beneficial owners.
  • Multi-year revenue, net profit, and balance sheet performance.
  • Compliance stamp: No pending insolvency or restructuring notices found in German court registries.

3. The "Executive Trace" (Director Background Check)

Enter the name of a business partner or managing director to see:

  • Every German company where they hold or held power of representation.
  • Historical health of their past ventures (solvent vs. liquidated).
  • Current co-directors and shared holding structures.

4. Automated Supply Chain / Loan Book Watchlist

Enterprise batch upload (CSV / API) enabling procurement and credit departments to:

  • Upload 500 to 10,000 corporate clients or suppliers at once.
  • Receive daily delta reports highlighting only entities with legal or management modifications.
  • Direct integration into Slack, Microsoft Teams, or webhook ingestion.
