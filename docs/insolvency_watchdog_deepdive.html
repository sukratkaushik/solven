Why right now is the best possible moment
Germany is in a decade-high insolvency wave. 81.6% of all insolvencies hit micro-enterprises with under 10 employees — exactly the clients freelancers work with. The average creditor loss per case is over €2 million. Freelancers and small suppliers are the most exposed and the least protected. Every one of those 23,900 insolvencies left a trail of unpaid invoices — and none of those creditors had any warning. That pain is real, current, and growing.
Target market
1.6 million freelancers in Germany. IT/SAP alone: ~26,000 registered SAP consultants in Germany. Daily rates €700–€1,200/day. One missed payment = months of lost income.
Your location
Walldorf is the centre of the SAP ecosystem. You are sitting in the single highest-concentration location for SAP freelancers in Europe. That's your warm network — people you work alongside every day who freelance on the side or know people who do.
Your advantage
You understand enterprise procurement. You know how SAP projects work, who gets paid, what a typical invoice looks like, and what "client goes under" means in practice. That credibility is invisible to a generic founder — visible to your first customers.
The gap
Free tools exist (manually checking handelsregister.de) but they require knowing the exact spelling, navigating 150 fragmented court databases, and doing it yourself every week. Nobody does it. That's your product.
Build the smallest thing that delivers the core promise: "know before your client can't pay you." Everything else is a distraction until you have 50 paying users.
Must build — no exceptions
Company search + add to watchlist
User types company name → searches handelsregister.ai API → sees results (name, city, legal form) → clicks to confirm → saved. Free plan: 3 companies. Paid: 25. This is the entire onboarding flow — it must feel instant and satisfying.
Daily insolvency check at 7am
Vercel cron job calls handelsregister.ai for every company on every user's watchlist. Checks the insolvency_publications endpoint. If non-empty → creates an alert row → sends email immediately. This is the product's entire value in one automated step.
Alert email — urgent, clear, actionable
Subject: "Insolvenz-Warnung: [Firma GmbH]". Body: company name, filing date, insolvency court name, case number, one-line plain-German summary, link to dashboard. Must land in inbox — not spam. Test with 10 real email addresses before launch.
Status dashboard
One page. List of watched companies. Each row: company name, city, status dot (green/amber/red), last checked date. That's it. No charts, no graphs, no complexity. If status is red → show the filing details inline.
Stripe subscription — one plan only
12 €/month. One plan. No annual vs monthly decision yet. Stripe Checkout handles everything — EU VAT, SEPA, failed payment retries. The upgrade prompt appears when a free user tries to add their 4th company.
Do not build yet
Director change alerts
Great feature — add it in v1.5 after your first 50 customers confirm they want it. Don't assume.
Risk scoring / credit rating
Your moat in v2. Requires combining multiple data sources. Too complex for MVP — ship alerts first.
Team accounts / CSV bulk import
This is your 29 €/month tier. Accountants and agencies need it. Build it at 100 paying users.
Mobile app
Email alerts work on every phone already. A native app adds nothing until you're at 500+ users.
The test for every feature you're tempted to add: does it make the core alert more reliable, or faster, or clearer? If not — it goes in the backlog.

Your moat over time
The API data is available to anyone. Your moat is not the data — it's the proprietary risk model you build over time by combining insolvency filings + late annual reports + director changes + Destatis sector data + Bundesbank credit trends into a single company risk score. That takes months of iteration with real users. Start simple, earn the right to build the model.
BYOB synergy
Your clothing brand project (BYOB) and this app are complementary, not competing. Insolvenz-Wächter generates recurring cash flow — steady, predictable monthly income — that funds your brand experiments without financial pressure. Build both. Let one fund the other.
